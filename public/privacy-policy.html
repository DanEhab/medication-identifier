Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Your Privacy Matters: Medication Identifier is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Product Queries: Pharmaceutical product names you type, or photographs of a pack you choose to take or upload
  • Patient Details: Name, age, sex and notes, if you choose to fill them in for a printed or exported report. Entirely optional.
  • Saved Items: Pharmaceutical products you choose to save, the names of any people you add to keep separate lists for, and any times or notes you set for a product

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Camera Access: Only while you are using the camera to read a pack. Frames are used to take the photograph you asked for and are not sent anywhere else.
  • Rate limiting: To stop the service being abused, our server keeps a counter against a salted, irreversible hash of your network address, together with the time. Your address itself is never stored, and the hash is not linked to your queries or to any identity.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT use analytics of any kind. The app contains no analytics or tracking software.
  • We do NOT collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or device and operating system details
  • We do NOT record which features you use
  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT access your contacts, your photo library beyond a file you pick, or other apps
  • We do NOT require an account, and we do NOT sell or share your data with anyone for advertising

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Primary Uses

  • Provide Product Information: Process your queries to fetch pharmaceutical product details from AI services
  • Enhance User Experience: Remember your user information across sessions
  • Cache Responses: Store product information to reduce API calls and improve speed
  • Generate Reports: Create PDF/Word documents when you request exports

2.2 Data Processing

  • Patient details, saved products, the people you add, times and notes, recent searches and your language and theme preferences are stored on your device only. They are never sent to us.
  • Product queries are sent to our servers and on to the Google Gemini API to be answered
  • As you type, the first letters are sent to our server to suggest products it has already answered for. Nothing is stored about who typed them.
  • Photographs are sent to be read and are NOT stored, by us or by Google
  • Answers are cached in our database against the product name for up to 180 days, so the next person asking about the same medicine gets a faster answer. The cache holds no information about you.
  • Checking your saved products against each other for possible interactions happens entirely on your device. That list is never sent anywhere.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage (Your Device)

  • Patient details for reports (name, age, sex, notes)
  • Saved products, and the names of any people you keep separate lists for
  • Times and notes you set for a product
  • Recent searches
  • Language and light or dark preference
  • This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear app data

3.2 Server Storage (MongoDB Database)

  • Product answers, keyed by the product name (cached for 180 days, then auto-deleted)
  • Search terms mapped to the product they turned out to mean, so a misspelling works next time
  • Short-lived rate-limiting counters against a salted, irreversible hash of a network address
  • No patient details, saved lists, or anything identifying you is stored on our servers

3.3 Security Measures

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • MongoDB database uses authentication and access controls
  • API keys are stored securely as environment variables
  • No payment or financial information is collected

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

4.1 Google Gemini API

  • Purpose: AI-powered pharmaceutical product identification and information retrieval
  • Data Shared: Product names, uploaded images (not stored by Google)
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4.2 MyMemory Translation API

  • Purpose: Translate product information to Arabic
  • Data Shared: English text for translation (no user info)
  • Privacy Policy: MyMemory Usage Terms

4.3 MongoDB Atlas

  • Purpose: Database for caching product information
  • Data Stored: Product search results only (no user data)
  • Privacy Policy: MongoDB Privacy Policy

4.4 Vercel (Hosting)

  • Purpose: Web hosting and serverless functions
  • Data Processed: API requests and responses
  • Privacy Policy: Vercel Privacy Policy

5. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

5.1 Right to Access

You can view all locally stored data in the app (user info, saved products)

5.2 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

  • Local Data: Clear app data or uninstall the app
  • Cached Data: Contact us to request deletion of cached product data

5.3 Right to Data Portability

Export your product information reports as PDF or Word documents

5.4 Right to Object

You can choose not to use features that process your data (e.g., don't upload images, don't fill in user info)

5.5 Right to Rectification

Edit or delete user information anytime within the app
