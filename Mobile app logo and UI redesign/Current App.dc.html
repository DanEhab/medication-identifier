Common Use

Atorvastatin lowers LDL ("bad") cholesterol and triglycerides in the blood, and raises HDL ("good") cholesterol.

It is prescribed to reduce the risk of heart attack and stroke in people with high cholesterol, and is usually taken alongside changes to diet and exercise.

Atorvastatin

Professional Information

Chemistry

Synthetic lipid-lowering agent. Calcium salt of a pentasubstituted pyrrole. Molecular weight 1155.34 g/mol; practically insoluble in water.

BCS Class

Class II — low solubility, high permeability.

Pharmacokinetics (ADME)

Absorption:
Rapid; T max 1–2 h. Absolute bioavailability ~14% due to pre-systemic clearance.
Distribution:
Vd ≈ 381 L; ≥98% plasma protein bound.
Metabolism:
CYP3A4 to active ortho- and para-hydroxylated metabolites.
